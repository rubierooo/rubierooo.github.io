Rubie - Like Bears

~ forthcoming 2026 ~

Rubie is a songstress, composer and multi-instrumentalist based in Glasgow. Winner of End of the Road's Next Wave competition and one of The Skinny's artists to watch in 2026, she is building a reputation for her highly theatrical live shows, tender and sprawling songwriting and eclectic DIY production.
Her forthcoming third album Like Bears is her most ambitious; across 10 glam inflected romps, sultry quasi bond themes and tearjerking campfire songs, it paints vivid pictures of the present apocalypse, from secret underground mutual aid networks to antifascist heist getaways.
Outside of her solo Material she plays keyboards for Brit Award Winning songwriter Jacob Alon, including appearances on the Graham Norton Show, on their Live At the Roundhouse live album, and opening for Florence and the Machine.
